Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Fenix ("the app") is built around a simple rule: your footage stays yours. This page explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and who to contact with questions.

What we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, basic account details (such as your email address) are handled through Supabase Auth to identify you and secure your account.
  • Video and audio content. Recordings you capture are processed on your device — audio is scanned locally for spoken keywords to route and edit your clips. Raw footage is not uploaded to our servers as part of this processing.
  • OAuth tokens for connected platforms. When you connect an account (e.g. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) so Fenix can publish on your behalf, we store the access/refresh tokens that platform issues. These tokens are used only to publish to the accounts you explicitly connected.

How your content is used

Video processing — keyword detection, editing, formatting — happens locally on your device. A finished clip is only uploaded to a platform if you've explicitly connected that platform's account and the clip was routed to it. Nothing is posted anywhere without an account connection you set up yourself.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, your footage, or your account information to anyone, for any reason.

Third-party platforms

When you connect YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or any other platform, that platform's own privacy policy also applies to what happens with content once it's published there. Fenix's access to those accounts is limited to the scopes needed to publish content on your behalf.

Data retention

Connected-platform tokens and account information are kept for as long as your account exists or until you disconnect a platform. Locally processed video/audio content is stored on your device and managed the same way any other file on your device is.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out at googledev@benscimediagroup.co.
