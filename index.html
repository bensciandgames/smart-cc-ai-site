What is Fenix?

Fenix is a video recording and auto-publishing app for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux. You record a video on your phone or desktop, and Fenix listens for a spoken keyword to know which platform and account it belongs to. It then automatically edits the clip to fit that platform, and uploads it directly to the account you've connected — YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram — with no manual exporting or re-uploading required.

Why Fenix asks for account access: when you connect a platform, Fenix requests only the permission needed to upload videos to that specific account on your behalf — for example, YouTube's upload permission to publish to your channel. Fenix never posts anything you haven't recorded yourself, and never accesses an account you haven't explicitly connected. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
